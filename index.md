## About Me

I work on **generalizable robot learning**: how can available experience become behavior that works in a new situation? My research connects reinforcement learning, visually grounded action, continuous execution, and human data for dexterous manipulation.

I am a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at Tongji University, advised by [Prof. Junqiao Zhao](http://cs1.tongji.edu.cn/~junqiao/). I also worked on reinforcement learning with [Prof. Eduardo Veas](https://eduveas.github.io/) during an Erasmus+ exchange at TU Graz.

I will join the **School of Computing and Data Science, The University of Hong Kong, as a PhD student in Fall 2027**, advised by [Prof. Hongyang Li](https://lihongyang.info/).
